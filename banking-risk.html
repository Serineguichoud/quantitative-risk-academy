What Makes This Course Different?

Risk management as it is used in modern banking.

Unlike many introductory courses, this programme approaches risk management from the perspective of modern banking and financial institutions. Rather than presenting isolated theoretical concepts, it explains how banks identify, quantify and manage financial risks while complying with international regulatory standards.

Throughout the programme, you will progressively develop the quantitative tools used by banks, regulators and financial institutions to measure risk, estimate potential losses, allocate regulatory capital and evaluate financial resilience under adverse economic conditions.

The course combines mathematical intuition, financial theory and real banking applications to provide a rigorous understanding of the methodologies underlying modern risk management.

What You Will Learn

The core frameworks used in banking risk management.

Banking and Financial Institutions

  • The role of banks within the financial system
  • Financial intermediation
  • Balance sheet transformation
  • Debt markets and banking activities
  • Sources of banking risk

Financial Risk Measurement

  • Value-at-Risk (VaR)
  • Expected Shortfall (ES)
  • Coherent risk measures
  • Tail risk
  • Introduction to Extreme Value Theory

Operational Risk

  • Basel definition of operational risk
  • Internal and external loss events
  • Loss Distribution Approach (LDA)
  • Monte Carlo simulation
  • Standardised Measurement Approach (SMA)

Credit Risk

  • Loan amortisation
  • Probability of Default (PD)
  • Loss Given Default (LGD)
  • Exposure at Default (EAD)
  • Expected Loss (EL)
  • Credit portfolio modelling
  • Vasicek model

Stress Testing

  • Scenario analysis
  • Historical and hypothetical stress tests
  • Structural and reduced-form approaches
  • Macroeconomic stress testing
  • Banking resilience assessment

ESG and Climate Risk

  • ESG investing
  • Sustainable finance
  • ESG ratings
  • Physical, transition and liability risks
  • Climate risk in financial institutions

Banking Regulation

  • Basel Committee
  • Basel I, II and III
  • Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA)
  • Regulatory capital
  • Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) approach
  • Counterparty credit risk
  • Netting and collateral
