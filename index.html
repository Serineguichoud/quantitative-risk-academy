Why This Training Format?

Most professional programmes in risk management are organised around a syllabus followed by an exam. Here, the approach is intentionally different.

The courses are built in a more human-sized format. Participants are guided throughout the programme, their progress can be followed regularly, and the final assessment takes the form of an applied project rather than a standardised exam.

  • 🤝Regular follow-up throughout the programme.
  • 📘Structured course material combined with practical exercises.
  • 🧠Training focused on understanding and application rather than exam preparation alone.
  • 📝A final project reviewed individually before the Certificate of Completion is issued.

About the Courses

The climate-risk programme is developed from original doctoral research conducted at Sorbonne University. The banking-risk programme is adapted from teaching material used in higher education in finance and risk management.

The goal is to provide rigorous, accessible and applied training for participants who want to build real understanding and not simply prepare for a standardised exam.
