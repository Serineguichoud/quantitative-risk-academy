Why this programme?

From climate data to financial risk.

Climate risk requires more than qualitative scenarios. This programme focuses on the quantitative methods used to measure extreme events, model economic impacts and analyse how shocks can propagate through interconnected systems.

01

Quantitative foundations

Develop a rigorous understanding of risk measures, distributions and the mathematical foundations used in climate risk analysis.

02

Climate risk modelling

Move from climate indicators to economic and financial impacts through quantitative modelling approaches.

03

Practical implementation

Apply the methods through exercises and modelling examples using modern quantitative tools.

About the course

Built from quantitative research and teaching.

The programme is developed from original doctoral research conducted at Sorbonne University and is designed for participants who want to understand climate risk through quantitative methods rather than qualitative frameworks alone.

Designed for researchers, economists, risk professionals and quantitative analysts.
Combines theory with practical exercises throughout the programme.
Connects statistical methods, network analysis, climate modelling and AI.
